What is OCIA?

The Order of Christian Initiation of Adults is the sacred process by which adults enter the Catholic Church. Rooted in the practice of the early Church, it is not a class to complete — it is a journey of conversion, accompanied by the parish community, guided by the Holy Spirit, and culminating in the Sacraments of Baptism, Confirmation, and the Holy Eucharist at the Easter Vigil. Here at Holy Family, this journey unfolds in the spirit of St. Francis of Assisi — with simplicity, joy, and a profound reverence for the presence of God in all things.

Stage I
Inquiry Seeking, questioning, exploring the faith
Stage II
Catechumenate Learning, praying, and growing in Christ
Stage III
Purification Lenten preparation and the Rite of Election
Stage IV
Mystagogy Living the Sacraments after Easter

About This App

This companion app was created for Holy Family OCIA candidates to support you between sessions — anytime, anywhere. It brings together everything you need to deepen your understanding of the faith: the Seven Sacraments, Sacred Scripture, essential prayers, the Rosary and chaplets, the lives of the saints, a step-by-step Mass guide, the Liturgical Year, and a Confirmation Saint Matcher. The ✦ Ask tab gives you direct access to a Catholic AI assistant grounded in the Catechism and Sacred Tradition.
